I've been deep in learning AI for a while now — agents, automation, the whole stack — and
most of what I've figured out has lived in scratch files, half-finished notes, and my own
head. That's fine for me today, useless for me in six months, and invisible to anyone else
figuring out the same things.

So: a running ledger. Short, unpolished entries, filed as I go. Some will be "here's how I
fixed X." Some will be half-formed ideas I'm still testing. All of it syndicates out from
here to wherever the conversation actually happens.

If you're building the same kind of thing, welcome. More soon.
