  The web copy the page loads: the file above scaled down to 512x512 (Lanczos, full-colour RGBA, no palette),

Rule kept for the G (read first-hand by the director on that page): full colour only, on white or black, clear
space of one G width, never modified or recoloured, use to refer to Google and never to imply endorsement. The
page, read again 2026-11-12: "Use the Google G to refer to Google (the company). For example, you can use it in news
articles or classroom teaching materials - so long as it's not being used for marketing or business purposes."
