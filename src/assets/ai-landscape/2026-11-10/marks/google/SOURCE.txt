Official mark. The page shows a smaller copy of it, scaled in proportion and changed in no other way.

source/GoogleG_FullColor_RGB.png
  Google G, full colour, the gradient version, as downloaded from the official guidelines page
  https://partnermarketinghub.withgoogle.com/brands/google/branding-guidelines/
  (its download link: storage.googleapis.com/pe-portal-consumer-prod-wagtail-static/original_images/GoogleG_FullColor_RGB.png)
  Fetched 2026-11-12. Byte-identical to the download. 2820x2820 RGBA PNG, 137,714 bytes.
  The G fills about a third of that canvas: the file carries its own clear space of one G width.
  It sits in source/ so that no page loads it. Nothing imports it.
  The same page offers GoogleG_FullColor_RGB.eps. No EPS-to-SVG converter was available on the build machine, so
  the plan's fallback applies: "Keep the PNG as google-g.png if no clean vector comes out of the EPS." There is
  therefore no google-g.svg.

google-g.png
  The web copy the page loads: the file above scaled down to 512x512 (Lanczos, full-colour RGBA, no palette),
  19,866 bytes. Same picture, same colours, same clear space. Not cropped, recoloured, redrawn or sharpened.
  The lid plate is drawn 512 pixels wide, so nothing on the page shows the mark larger than this copy.
  To remake it: sharp(source).resize(512, 512, { kernel: 'lanczos3' }).png({ compressionLevel: 9, palette: false }).

gemini-spark.svg is NOT shipped. Plan rule: "Google says product icons need a Partner Marketing Hub account and an
approval form. Owner decision needed (SPEC 10). Until then the Google G is the lid mark and this file is not
shipped."

Rule kept (read first-hand by the director on that page): full colour only, on white or black, clear space of one
G width, never modified or recoloured, use to refer to Google and never to imply endorsement.
