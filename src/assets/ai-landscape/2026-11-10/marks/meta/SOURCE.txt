No Meta mark is shipped in this folder. Both planned files are missing on purpose.

meta-logo.svg (Meta logo, primary variation) - NOT SHIPPED
  Official source: https://www.meta.com/brand/resources/meta/company-brand/ (page "Updated June 2026", read
  2026-11-12). Its download (Meta_Company-Lockup.zip) sits behind the checkbox "I have read and accept the
  applicable guidelines and other terms for use." Accepting Meta's terms is the owner's decision, not the art
  builder's, so the file was not downloaded. The same page says to "work closely with your Meta counterpart for
  detailed guidelines and approvals through the internal Brand Review process" (SPEC 10 open item).
  Fallback checked: Wikimedia Commons "File:Meta Platforms Inc. logo.svg" (public domain, trademarked, credit
  about.meta.com/brand/resources/meta/company-brand). It was not used: the file itself says "Graphic created by"
  a Commons contributor, so it is a third-party recreation, not Meta's file.
  To ship it: the owner accepts the terms on that page, downloads the zip, and the primary-colour SVG goes here
  unaltered as meta-logo.svg.
  Rule to keep then (read first-hand by the director): primary colour variation where possible, never under 12 px,
  clear space of twice the symbol height, never to represent Facebook. Editorials are a listed use; the page also
  says all use needs approval.

muse-mark.svg (Muse mark, the blue looping M) - NOT SHIPPED
  Seen in the header of https://muse.ai/ on 2026-11-12; no public download exists. Plan rule: "Ship it only if an
  official vector file can be obtained. Never redraw or trace it. Without the file, print "Muse" in the house type
  beside the Meta logo."

Until a file arrives, print the names in house type.
