I've been deep in learning AI for a while now, from agents (AI that goes off and does a job on
its own) to automation. Most of what I've figured out has lived in scratch files, half-finished
notes and my own head. That's fine for me today, but it's useless to me in six months and
invisible to anyone else figuring out the same things.

So it goes here now, in a running log I call [the ledger](/ledger/). Short, unpolished entries,
filed as I go. Some will be "here's how I fixed X." Some will be half-formed ideas I'm still
testing.

If you're building the same kind of thing, welcome.
