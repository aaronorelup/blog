Wind it all the way back and play it again from the start?

Touch or click the light to begin. Drag a ring to turn it, or tap a ring to click it on by one. With a keyboard, Up and Down choose a ring, Left and Right turn it, Space clicks it on, and M mutes the sound. Nothing is timed and nothing can go wrong.
