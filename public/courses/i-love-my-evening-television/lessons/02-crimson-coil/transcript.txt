[0:46] 02 The painter you never see

The host is never seen: no face, no hands, only his brush, his voice and the subtitles. Aaron cast him as a homage to a famous public-television painter; and as a working rule he is never named and never impersonated. Paintings speak to him, and he answers with total courtesy, as if it happens all the time. He gives the last stroke away, and his last clean word, said softly to the viewer, is Goodnight. [4:19] 07 A bell every five minutes
