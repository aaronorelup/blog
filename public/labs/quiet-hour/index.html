    About this film

    What you are watching

    A dark living room near midnight. On the old TV a calm maker, seen only as two hands, teaches a small music box to play jigs by ear, and at 20:00 on the programme clock turns it up to the limit it has just measured. Nothing on this page is a video: your browser draws every frame from the frame number and the ending you chose.

    What is real and what is designed

    Every number in the picture and every event comes from the log of a small neural network's training run on old fiddle tunes, the one published as Fable 5.2's version in the post, in its four endings. The room, the hands, the camera, the voice and its words, the thresholds that turn numbers into pictures, and a few fixed moments (the title card, the opening line, the taps every five minutes, the chalk at 20:00, Goodnight) are designed, the same in every ending.

    The marks on the time bar are what the rules make happen in the ending you picked. Switch endings and watch them move. Keys: Space plays and pauses, the arrow keys step 5 seconds.

    The recipe

    • make/README.txt: how the film is rendered and checked frame by frame
    • film.js: one frame, as a function of (frame, ending)
    • src/signals.js and src/rules.js: every number and every rule
    • src/shots.js, src/programme/, src/room/: the shots, the TV picture, the room
    • src/player.js: this page
