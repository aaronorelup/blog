Things to try

Each of these is one idea for one chapter, built as its own small page so it can be opened, scrolled and played with before it goes into the working copy.

The toy boxFor the 3D chapter: a box that bursts open, and every different Jefrie and a few other toys tumble out. Pick them up, throw them, knock them over. First version. The tea house, taken apartFor the chapter about this website: the homepage tea house seen from above, coming apart piece by piece as you scroll. Storm Bell Fighter as a living backdropFor the games chapter: the game's own painted layers, rain, fires and both fighters as one 3D scene that answers the mouse.

The little television, painted (a candidate, not in use)

The set in the working copy is the 3D one from look B. This painted version was made so the set would stay sharp when the camera goes through its glass. It waits for a yes or a no. The first try, the fixes, and the model beside the painting.

Sliding paper screens between chapters (tried, dropped)

Two sliding screens that shut on one chapter and open on the next. Dropped on 13 November: the changes between chapters stay as look B does them, with its zoom and its slide over to night.

Paper cards (tried, dropped)

Washi paper with a hand-cut edge and pictures set behind a hole cut in the sheet. Dropped on 13 November: the cards will follow the homepage, clean and digital.

The engine test page (parked)

A rebuilt scroll engine with placeholder rooms: the garden, the door, the screens, the index, a phone layout and a version with nothing moving. It is parked while the working copy is developed from look B. These are the frames looked at in each round of fixing.

Scrolling fast, then checking the picture

The early looks could be left half-changed after fast scrolling. The engine test page is scrolled hard with a real mouse wheel and the result is compared with the same place loaded fresh. These are pairs from that test.
