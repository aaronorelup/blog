Daily candy · MiniMax-H3 interior keyframes · Qwen-Image 2.1 stills

Several mid-clip keyframes, no first or last frame, for fast fights

With two or three MiniMaxH3AddGuide stills and no end frames, the frame closest to each keyframe was the asked frame for all 7 keyframes in trials 1, 2 and 4. The same seed and prompt with no guides drew other character designs in every trial with characters, yet still hit some of the moments from the words alone. Around each keyframe the guided clips slow almost to a stop.

Experiment. 2-3 MiniMaxH3AddGuide stills at interior frames, no first or last frame, fast character and camera motion. Each keyframe is framed where the prompt's timed camera move is at that second, and the prompt names what it adds. Every trial has a same-seed, same-prompt arm with no guides.

Hypothesis. Unguided H3 misses the designed moments (checklist written before any job); guided hits each within ±2 frames, without CNDY-040 trial 2's whip-out snap, looking no worse than unguided.

Models. MiniMax-H3 (fl2va + turbo 4-step, 736×416) is the subject. Keyframes: Qwen-Image 2.1 multi-ref t2i at 1472×832 (character sheets + one set plate per trial); trial 3's are CPU composites of the Episode 2 sheet and desk plate.

Look for. In each side-by-side, the header turns red within ±2 frames of a guide. At each keyframe: is the guided side worse (mush, limbs)? Is there a snap into or out of it? In trial 3, does VOID come early? In trial 4 C, is there a freeze or stutter between f84 and f96?

The hypothesis, part by part, and what went differently

Unguided misses the moments: trial 1 hero inversion yes (f85-135), vault no, clash partly, no orbit; trial 2 both waypoints yes, its high shot steeper; trial 3 stamped at f166 on another sheet; trial 4 no blade through his chest. Every unguided arm with characters drew other designs; trial 3's drew another sheet. Within ±2: offset 0 for 7 of 7 in trials 1, 2, 4 B; trial 3 VOID at f164-165 against the stamp at f168; trial 4 C's strike spans f78-103 (minimum f99). Snap: no step met the rule (>15 and >3× median) in trials 1-3, a strict rule that 040's own whip would only partly have met; like for like, the ±5 MAE around the guides is 6.7-10.3 in trial 1 and 20.3 / 21.6 at trial 2's f95, against 040's 53.3 / 37.4. Trial 4's lunge steps are in all arms. No worse: see the asked±2 sheets.

Not as planned: trial 1's first keyframe set was dropped (features crossed) and re-made with a cast sentence, so trial 1 took 21 min of GPU; the setup was reusable, so trials 2-4 ran (2,767 s in all). Trials 1 and 4 used Jefrie's setting canon as the set plate. Trial 2's keyframes came back less extreme than asked and were kept. Trial 4's strike was re-seeded once. No stick figure was used.
