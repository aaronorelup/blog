# The two prototypes' notes, and the site's tokens

Copies kept for the tea room's builders (`docs/TEA_ROOM.md`). The two looks themselves are frozen and live at
`/labs/tea-room/a/` and `/labs/tea-room/b/` (`public/labs/tea-room/`).

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `look-a-NOTES.md`, `look-b-NOTES.md` | each prototype's rules and its rounds of art-director notes |
| `look-a-GEN_LOG.md`, `look-b-GEN_LOG.md` | every picture generated for it, with the prompt that worked |
| `tokens.md` | the site's colours and type as measured facts |

They are copied as written, with three changes: the name of the image model is left out of the two logs and of
A's notes ("the image model"), and the name of the branch the tokens were read on is left out of `tokens.md`.
Paths such as `gen/LOG.md`, `gen/raw/` and `kit/` are the prototypes' own working folders, which are not in this
repo.
